### Changelog

### 2026 04 13

- [Virtual currency support](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-04-13/virtual-currency-support.md): Coupons, items, store items, and daily rewards now support a new `vc` (virtual currency) type.
- [Webhook analytics events and batch delivery](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-04-13/webhook-analytics-batch.md): Webhooks now support analytics events for checkout and hub interactions, plus a new `batch.ready` event.

### 2026 05 01

- [Highlights field for items](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-05-01/highlights-field.md): A new optional `highlights` field has been added to all item types — regular items, lootboxes, subscriptions, and virtual currencies — enabling structured benefit descriptions to be stored and returned alongside each item.
- [payment_number deprecated, receipt_number added](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-05-01/payment-number-deprecation.md): The `payment_number` field in get_payments responses has been deprecated and replaced by a new required `receipt_number` field.

### 2026 05 07

- [Apple-specific image URL fields on items](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-05-07/apple-image-urls.md): Five Apple-specific image URL fields are now exposed on item responses across all item-bearing endpoints: `image_url_apple`, `image_url_featured_apple`, `icon_url_apple`, `background_image_url_apple`, and `featured_card_background_image_url_apple`.

### 2026 05 08

- [Unified item schema in order and payment responses](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-05-08/order-payment-item-schema.md): Item objects in order and payment responses now share a single `Item` schema.
- [Page card button text and custom URL](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-05-08/page-card-button-and-url.md): Two new optional fields are available on pages: `button_text` (the label shown on the page card button) and `custom_url` (a URL the card links to instead of the page itself).

### 2026 05 21

- [Unified item source enum across catalog and order responses](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-05-21/item-source-unification.md): The two per-domain `ItemSource` schemas (one for catalog items, one for order items) have been consolidated into a single shared `ItemSource` enum.
- [Category templates and video thumbnail toggle on page blocks](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-05-21/page-block-extensions.md): Page layouts gain two new authoring options:
- [Bonus quantity fields on item responses](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-05-21/quantity-bonus-fields.md): Public item responses now expose two new optional fields, `quantity_bonus_value` and `quantity_after_bonus`, describing the bonus quantity added on top of an item's base quantity and the resulting total.
- [Store promotion scheduling fields are now optional and nullable](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-05-21/store-promotion-nullable-schedule.md): `start_at` and `end_at` on store promotions are no longer required and may be `null`.

### 2026 05 22

- [Reward eligibility and virtual currency rewards on order responses](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-05-22/order-reward-fields.md): Order responses now expose three new optional fields describing the rewards a player is eligible to receive for an order: `eligible_for_reward_points` (integer), `eligible_for_loyalty_points` (integer), and `rewards`, a structured object listing the available virtual currency rewards along with each reward's issue status.

### 2026 05 25

- [RemoveFromSegmentNode for campaign and segment graphs](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-05-25/remove-from-segment-node.md): Campaign and segment-group graphs gain a new `RemoveFromSegmentNode` action node, the counterpart to the existing `AddToSegmentNode`.

### 2026 05 27

- [Multi-item coupon rewards](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-05-27/coupon-multi-item-rewards.md): Coupons can now grant multiple items in a single redemption.

### 2026 05 29

- [Progressive Web App (PWA) support](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-05-29/pwa-support.md): Campaign automation gains end-to-end support for Progressive Web Apps.

### 2026 06 04

- [Creators resource](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-06-04/creators-resource.md): A new Creators resource adds full CRUD management for content creators in a creator program.

### 2026 06 08

- [Cashback Rewards resource removed](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-06-08/cashback-rewards-removed.md): The Cashback Rewards resource has been removed.

### 2026 06 10

- [Creator annotations](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-06-10/creator-annotation.md): Creator responses now include an optional `annotation` object, backed by the new `CreatorAnnotation` schema.

### 2026 06 12

- [Coupon limits for combined store-item discounts and bonuses](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-06-12/coupon-store-item-limits.md): Coupons gained two optional fields — `store_item_discount_limit_percent` and `store_item_bonus_limit_percent` — that cap how much a coupon can stack on top of a store item's own discount or bonus.

### 2026 06 13

### 2026 06 16

- [Legacy single-item coupon fields removed](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-06-16/coupon-legacy-item-fields-removed.md): The deprecated single-item coupon fields — `item_id`, `quantity`, and the read-only `item` object — have been removed from the coupon API.

### 2026 06 18

- [Checkout payment-method webhook event renamed](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-06-18/checkout-payment-method-event-renamed.md): The webhook event type `analytics.checkout.payment_method.open` has been renamed to `analytics.checkout.payment_method.select`.

### 2026 06 19

- [Checkout payment-method webhook event renamed to `select`](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-06-19/checkout-payment-method-event-renamed.md): The webhook event type `analytics.checkout.payment_method.selected` has been renamed to `analytics.checkout.payment_method.select`.
- [Filter creators by country](https://docs.aghanim.com/s2s-api/changelog/2026-06-19/creator-country-filtering.md): You can now filter creators by country and discover which countries are available.

### 2026 06 20

### 2026 07 01

## Webhooks

- [Analytics Webhooks](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/analytics-events.md): Aghanim's analytics webhooks notify your game of frontend-only player interactions on the game hub and checkout page.
- [Webhook Batch Export](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/batching.md): Instead of delivering events directly via webhook requests, Aghanim collects events over a time window, stores them as a JSONL file, and sends a `batch.ready` webhook notification with a pre-signed URL to download the batch.
- [Webhooks Changelog](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/changelog.md): - Added optional `payment_currency` to order events (order.created, order.paid, order.canceled, order.refunded) — the currency the player was actually charged in
- [Coupon Redeemed Webhook](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/coupon-redeemed.md): Aghanim offers a coupon redeemed webhook to notify your game when a redeem code for a free item has been used by a player, providing detailed information about the event.
- [Fraud Reported Webhook](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/fraud.md): When a payment processor reports fraud on a completed payment, Aghanim sends a `fraud.reported` webhook to notify your game.
- [In-Game Popup Webhook](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/in-game-popup.md): Through Aghanim's LiveOps Builder, developers can trigger in-game popups in response to player actions or interactions within the Aghanim-generated game hub.
- [Item Add Webhook](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/item-add.md): Aghanim offers a centralized webhook that alerts your game to credit items to a player's account based on their activities within the game hub.
- [Item Remove Webhook](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/item-remove.md): Aghanim offers a centralized webhook that alerts your game to remove items from a player's account.
- [Marketing Consent Webhooks](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/marketing-consent.md): Aghanim's marketing consent webhooks notify your game about changes to a player's marketing consent, allowing you to keep your marketing lists in sync.
- [Mobile Push Webhook](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/mobile-push.md): Through Aghanim's LiveOps Builder, developers can trigger mobile push notifications in response to player actions or interactions within the Aghanim-generated game hub.
- [Order Canceled Webhook](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/order-canceled.md): Aghanim utilizes an order canceled webhook to inform you of a transaction reversed by a bank or a payment system, providing detailed information about the event.
- [Order Created Webhook](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/order-created.md): Aghanim's order created webhook notifies your game when a new order is created.
- [Order Paid Webhook](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/order-paid.md): Aghanim's order paid webhook notifies your game of successfully completed payments on the game hub, providing detailed information about the transaction.
- [Webhook Overview](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/overview.md): Aghanim uses webhooks to notify your game about player-triggered events within the Aghanim-generated game hub.
- [Payment Webhooks](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/payment-events.md): When a player attempts to pay for an order, a payment is created.
- [Order Refunded Webhook](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/refund.md): Aghanim utilizes a refund webhook to inform your game of a refund initiated in the Transactions section of the Aghanim Dashboard, providing detailed information about the event.
- [Store Get Webhook](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/store-get.md): The `store.get` webhook retrieves a list of items available for a player in the Game Hub store.
- [Subscription Webhooks](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/subscription.md): Aghanim's subscription webhooks notify your game about subscription lifecycle events, allowing you to manage player access to subscription-based content and services.
- [Player Update Webhook](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/update-player.md): The `player.update` webhook is triggered when a player's profile is updated on the Game Hub.
- [Player Verify Webhook](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/verify-player.md): Aghanim utilizes a player verification webhook to inform your game about player logins, requiring confirmation from your webhook server to permit or deny access to the game hub.

### Changelog

### 2026 05 13

- [Richer context on item events](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/changelog/2026-05-13/item-add-more-context.md): Item events carry more attribution context.

- [Error responses for player.verify](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/changelog/2026-06-19/player-verify-error-response.md): Your game may now return a lenient flat error body — `{ status, code, message }` — when it rejects a `player.verify` webhook.

### 2026 06 25

- [Payment currency on order events](https://docs.aghanim.com/webhooks/changelog/2026-06-25/order-payment-currency.md): Order webhook events now include an optional `payment_currency` field — the currency the player was actually charged in, which can differ from the order's base `currency`.
