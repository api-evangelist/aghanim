If you're experiencing performance issues or bugs in the game, here's how to get help: Contact Game Support : Most games have a built-in feature for reporting issues directly to the support team. Look for options like "Report an Issue" or "Help" in the game’s settings or help menu. Visit the Official Website : If you can’t find the in-game support feature, visit the game’s official website or its social media pages listed on the Game Hub.
