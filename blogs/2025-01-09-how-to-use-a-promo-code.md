Types of promo codes On the Hub, there are three types of promo codes you can redeem: Discount Coupons Bonus Coupons Coupons for Free Items Make sure you know which type of coupon you have so you can follow the right steps. Redeeming a free item Open ‘Redeem Code: In the Hub’s left-hand menu, choose “Redeem Code.” (You may also see other ways to get to this page, depending on the game). Enter the Code: Type your promo code in the field provided and click “Redeem.
